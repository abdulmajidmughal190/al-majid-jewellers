Har design ke saath available wazan aur gold purity likhi jayegi. Stone/beads ya doosre parts hon to unki details order confirm karne se pehle alag se pooch kar verify karein.

🕒 Hourly Rate Updates

Gold rate har ghante badal sakta hai. Website par rate shop admin ka latest manually entered rate hai, automatic market feed nahi. Final rate tab confirm/lock samjha jayega jab customer final amount ki tasdeeq kare, 75% advance shop ko receive ho, aur shop WhatsApp par payment aur rate lock ki likhit tasdeeq bheje.

📦 Packing aur Order Record

Order confirm hone par customer ko design, wazan, purity, final amount, advance/remaining payment aur andazati delivery details WhatsApp par likhit taur par bheji jayengi. Payment receipt aur order reference sambhal kar rakhein.

💬 Direct Customer Support

Order se pehle sawal pooch sakte hain. Shop se final details WhatsApp par confirm karein. Product photos asal design ke mutabiq rakhne ki koshish ki jayegi; rang/finishing screen ki wajah se thori mukhtalif nazar aa sakti hai.

🔒 Privacy

Naam, phone number, address aur order details order process, delivery aur zaroori customer support ke liye istemal honge. Personal information ghair zaroori taur par share nahi ki jayegi.

⭐ Customer Reviews

Order, Payment & Return Policy

Order confirm karne se pehle payment aur return terms zaroor parhein.

💳 Payment Plan

75% advance payment order confirm karte waqt deni hogi. Baqi 25% payment order aap tak pohanchne par ada karni hogi, agreed delivery/checking process ke mutabiq.

↩️ Return / Refusal

Agar parcel check karne ke baad customer item qabool na kare, to return/deduction policy apply hogi. 24K gold: 1 tola = 16 aanay; 3 aanay deduction (3/16 hissa). Final hisaab shop confirm karegi.

⚖️ 22K & 18K Gold

22K aur 18K ke saman ki deduction fixed nahi. Shop item dekh kar deduction tay karegi aur order se pehle WhatsApp par exact hisaab bata kar razamandi legi.

Gold rate har ghante badal sakta hai. Rate final amount confirm karne aur 75% advance receive hone par confirm/lock hoga. Order se pehle tamam terms WhatsApp par tasdeeq karein. Local consumer-protection qawaneen aur courier/payment terms verify karein.
